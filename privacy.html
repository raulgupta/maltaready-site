MaltaReady – Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026 · Last updated 20 September 2026

MaltaReady is a mobile app that teaches practical Maltese to workers moving to Malta. It is published by Rahul Gupta, New Delhi, India ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: the app works offline, your voice recordings never leave your phone, and the only information we receive is what is needed to activate the full course.

1. Data we do not collect

  • Voice recordings. When you use the pronunciation checker, the recording is processed entirely on your phone by a speech model that runs inside the app. Recordings are not uploaded, not stored on our servers, and are deleted from the phone when you record again or leave the screen.
  • Personal details. The app has no account, no sign-up, and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts.
  • Advertising or analytics. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

2. Data we receive

  • Activation. If you unlock the full course with an activation code, the app sends the code and a random device identifier generated by the app to our activation server, so that the code can be tied to your device and cannot be shared. We keep the code, the device identifier and the activation date. This identifier is not your phone's serial number or advertising ID and cannot identify you personally.
  • In-app purchase. If you unlock the full course through the App Store or Google Play, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We receive a purchase confirmation and no payment details.
  • Speech model download. On first use the app downloads a 71 MB speech-recognition model file from our hosting provider. This is an ordinary file download; we do not collect anything from it beyond standard server logs (IP address, time), which are kept for at most 30 days for security.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone – only when you tap Record on the Practice screen, to compare your pronunciation with the native speaker. You can refuse it; the rest of the app works without it.
  • Internet – to download the speech model once and to activate a code. All lessons and audio work offline.

4. Data on your phone

Your progress, language choice and activation state are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

5. Children

The app is intended for adults preparing to work in Malta and does not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your rights

You can ask us what activation data we hold for your device and ask for it to be deleted by contacting the address below. Deleting it deactivates the course on that device.
