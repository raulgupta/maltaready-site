Common questions

Does the app need internet? Only twice: once to download the speech model for the pronunciation score (71 MB), and once if you activate a code. Lessons, audio and practice work offline.

The pronunciation score says "basic score". The speech model has not finished downloading. Connect to Wi-Fi and open the Practice screen; the download resumes by itself.

I have an activation code. Open More → Unlock the full course → enter the code. A code works on one phone.

I changed my phone. Email us the code and we will move it to the new phone.

Which languages are included? Maltese with meanings in English, Hindi, Malayalam and Nepali, all recorded by a native Maltese speaker.
