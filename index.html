MaltaReady

Learn practical Maltese and get ready to work in Malta. Human-recorded Maltese, meanings in English, Hindi, Malayalam and Nepali, an offline pronunciation checker, and a plain-language guide to permits, documents and rights. Works offline.

Independent app. MaltaReady is made by Rahul Gupta and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the Government of Malta or any government agency. The in-app Malta guide summarises public information and links to the page each topic is based on. Sources: identita.gov.mt, jobsplus.gov.mt, dier.gov.mt, health.gov.mt, servizz.gov.mt, nursesandmidwivescouncil.gov.mt, legislation.mt, mfhea.mt, skillspass.org.mt.
